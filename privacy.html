Orbito

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Orbito (the "game"), made by OneEyedGames ("we", "us"), handles information when you play it. Questions: CONTACT_EMAIL.

In short

  • There is no account and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads. Our advertising partners collect some device information to show and measure ads. On first launch you choose whether ads may be personalised.
  • Purchases go through Google Play (or the App Store). We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device: credits, rocket upgrades, best scores, unlocked planets and cosmetics, settings, your answer to the ads question, and whether you bought "Remove ads". This data stays on your device. We do not receive it. It is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising

The game shows rewarded ads (only when you choose to watch one, for example to double your credits) and occasional full-screen ads between runs. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies, including ironSource) and the ad networks it works with.

To deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure results and prevent fraud, these partners may collect from your device:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where available);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information such as model, operating system, language, screen size, network type and app version;
  • how you interact with ads (for example views and clicks).

Your choice: on first launch the game asks whether to allow personalised ads. If you allow them, partners may use this information to show ads based on your interests. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. Partners still process some data, such as the IP address, to deliver and measure them.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings: on Android, Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads); on iOS, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

These partners handle data under their own privacy policies. See the Unity Privacy Policy, which covers Unity Ads, LevelPlay and ironSource.

In-app purchases

Purchases ("Remove ads" and cosmetic items) are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not receive your name, card details or billing address. The game only receives confirmation of the purchase so it can unlock the item and restore it on request.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game in a way that resulted in data being collected, contact us and we will help you with the advertising partner involved.

Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region with data protection laws (for example the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to or restrict its processing. We do not hold personal data about you ourselves. For data processed by our advertising partners, use the options in their privacy policies above, or contact us and we will help. You can withdraw ad personalisation consent at any time through your device's ad settings, or by uninstalling and reinstalling the game and answering the question again.

Security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the game. Data collected by advertising partners is kept for the periods described in their policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
